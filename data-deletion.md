# Data deletion — Esperanza Football

Esperanza Football does not create accounts for families or fans. You can use
the public roster, schedule, news, and sponsor features without providing a
name, email address, or other profile information.

## Push notification data

If you opted in to push notifications, OneSignal stores a device push token
and subscription state for notification delivery.

1. To stop notifications, turn off **Push notifications** in the app's
   Settings or revoke notification permission in your device settings.
2. To request deletion of the OneSignal subscription record, email
   **tenysonpartridge@gmail.com** with the subject
   **"Esperanza Football app: data deletion"**. Include your platform (iOS or
   Android), device model, and the approximate date you enabled notifications
   so the record can be located. We may ask for additional information if
   those details do not identify a unique subscription.

## Administrator account data

Program staff administrators can request deletion of their Supabase
authentication account and administrator authorization. Send the request from
the email address used to sign in to **tenysonpartridge@gmail.com** with the
subject **"Esperanza Football app: admin account deletion"**.

Public team content is maintained on behalf of the football program rather
than as an administrator's personal account data. If a roster entry or other
public program content needs correction or removal, identify that content in
the request so program staff can review it.

We will acknowledge deletion requests and complete verified requests within
30 days. See the [privacy policy](./privacy/) for more information.
